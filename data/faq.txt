Document 1: Order Tracking
Customers can track an order using the tracking link
sent to their registered email address. If the link does
not work, contact customer support with the order ID.

Document 2: Returns
Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery
if the item meets the return policy conditions. The
customer should provide the order ID and reason for return.

Document 3: Refunds
After an approved return is received and inspected,
the refund is processed to the original payment method.
The time taken depends on the payment provider.

Document 4: Account Access
If a customer cannot sign in, they should use the
Forgot Password option and follow the email instructions.
If they do not receive an email, they should check spam.
